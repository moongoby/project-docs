- `:80` VirtualHost:

  - `<Location /api/health/>` → `http://127.0.0.1:8001/api/health/`
- `:443` VirtualHost:
  - `<Location /api/health>` → `http://127.0.0.1:8001/api/health`
  - `<Location /api/health/>` → `http://127.0.0.1:8001/api/health/`
